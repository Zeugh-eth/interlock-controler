Haiku never reached the grasp. The tools could not find the carton from the height the rules demand, and the rules offered no other path.

Two Haiku runs on the same arm, scene and MCP ended with zero jaw closes after 41 tool calls. The carton was grabbed on the next run in 18 calls once the agent judged the raw camera frame itself and set aside three of the written rules. Most of the gap is tooling and instructions, not model size: the five changes under “Do now” would have let Haiku’s own first plan succeed.

Verdict, in order of weight

  1. The detector cannot see this carton from above 6 cm. YOLO-World returned nothing for it at 8, 10.8, 11.5, 12 and 14 cm with every prompt tried; it first appeared at 5 cm (conf 0.19). analyze_object requires a detection at survey height, so its precondition is unsatisfiable here, and rule 1 (“never approach from a close-up”) forbids the only path that works.
  2. analyze_object’s search makes things worse when it fails: it pans the arm to absolute +35° / −40° and leaves it there, accepts detections of any confidence (a 0.069 “carton” that was the leader arm’s gripper got a full plan), and its reach fallbacks move the tool away from the base, away from the object.
  3. Survey heights of 11–12 cm are unreachable at x < 18 cm, and refusals say “tilt 10 deg” without saying what is reachable. Fable did the whole approach between z 8 and z 2, never above 8.
  4. The skill text has no manual route and no definition of “attempt”. Haiku obeyed rule 1 literally twice, rejected a (wrong) plan for the wrong reason, and stopped after exploration failures with zero closes, as rule 7 told it to.
  5. Model limits showed, but only after the tooling failed: Haiku did not recognise the carton sitting under the cross at pan −38°, never thought to lower the arm when 12 cm was refused, and anchored on numbers in my brief (“raise to 12 cm”) that were themselves wrong.

Root causes

Tooling (arm-daemon/skills.py, camview.py, mcp_server.py)

T1blocking

Detector blind spot above 6 cm. YOLO-World misses this carton at every survey height; it appears only from z 5 down (0.19) and z 3.4 (0.31). analyze_object cannot return a plan without a detection, and grab() aligns on detector offsets at approach z 6, where it would also see nothing (off is None → not_aligned).

Evidence: run 1 call 8 (“detections: none” at 8 cm); run 2 looks at 11.5–11.8 cm; Fable’s looks at 8.0 and 6.6 cm (none), 5.0 (carton 0.19), 3.4 (0.31).

T2blocking

No confidence floor in planning. _best_detection takes the highest-confidence box whatever its value; the overlay hides < 0.15 but the planner does not. A 0.069 “carton” (the leader arm’s gripper, 25 cm to the right) got full_view: true and a feasible plan with roll 90 to (0.195, −0.253).

Evidence: run 1 call 9; frame 1 below. Haiku refused it for an unrelated reason. A model that trusted the card would have driven the gripper into the leader arm.

T3blocking

Pan scan is absolute, wide and one-way. PAN_SCAN = (35, −40) writes absolute servo angles regardless of the current pan or where the object was last seen, and after a failed scan the arm stays at −40°. Every failed analyze left the arm pointing 40° away from the carton, so Haiku’s next look showed an unrelated scene.

Evidence: run 1 calls 3, 10, 11 (tool y −0.117 then +0.118 after scans).

T4blocking

Survey height unreachable near the base; fallbacks go the wrong way; refusals name no alternative. Top-down IK rejects z 0.11–0.12 at x < 0.18 (tilt > 12°). The fallbacks try x 0.24, 0.22, 0.20: further from the base, away from an object at x 0.16. goto_xyz/move_tool answer “unreachable top-down: tilt 10 deg” and nothing else; z ≤ 0.085 at x 0.16 is fine (Fable’s whole approach).

Evidence: 4 refusals across the Haiku runs, all at z ≥ 0.11; zero refusals in the Fable run at z ≤ 0.08.

T5misleading

Overlay centimetres assume the table plane. The carton top is ~5 cm above the model table, so sizes and offsets read 1.5–2× too large. The look-down overlay said fwd −7.6, left +3.8 cm; the grasp happened at dx −4.0 and a net dy of −1.3 cm. HEIGHT_BY_LABEL exists but is not applied to the offsets, and the “(on table)” caveat is a footnote a small model takes literally.

Evidence: frame 3 below; Haiku run 2 drifted 4.4 cm left on the overlay’s numbers.

T6missing

“Between the jaws” is not drawn anywhere. The judgement that worked lives in the raw frame: fixed jaw = raw-left (base side), moving jaw = raw-right and off-frame when open, the object must sit just right of the fixed jaw tip with ~1 cm gap at the jaw-tip row. look() defaults to the rotated overlay whose cross is a model estimate (4 cm off in demo 053114); look(raw=True) has no annotations at all.

Evidence: frames 4–6 below; the 053114 notes.

T7minor

Instruction/tool mismatch. Server rule 5 says grasp(label=...); the MCP signature is grasp(close_pct, lift_cm). Following the rule literally raises a validation error.

T8in progress

Auto cards mislabel aborted and place-only runs (closed_empty with lift/carry phases; never_closed + failed for a successful put-down). Being fixed in the linked session.

Instructions (.claude/skills/driving-the-arm/SKILL.md, server INSTRUCTIONS)

I1

Rule 1 is absolute and has no fallback. “analyze_object from 10 cm or higher until full_view … Never approach from a close-up.” With T1 this is a dead end, and no manual route is described. Haiku obeyed it twice and even rejected a full_view card because 8.7 < 10.

I2

“Three attempts, then stop” defines neither an attempt nor an exploration budget. Haiku counted exploration failures as attempts and stopped with zero closes. A small model needs: attempt = jaw close; a move budget (say 12) before asking; and “if you can see it at look-down, descend and look before giving up”.

I3

The facts that made the grab work are not in the skill. Which jaw is fixed; raw-frame axes; open 55 % before descending; objects on top of something read larger and farther; reach envelope (keep z ≤ 9 cm when x < 18 cm); the overlay cross can be 4 cm off. Fable had them from memory notes and the demo frames.

Model

M1

Recognition under rotation and scale. At pan −37.7°, z 10.8, the carton sat directly under the cross (frame 2) and Haiku did not recognise it. It did recognise it at look-down every time.

M2

No spatial reasoning past the refusal. Reachable at 8 cm, refused at 11.6 cm: the inference “go lower” was never made. It also never opened the gripper or descended, so parallax and heights never came up.

M3

Anchoring on the brief. It executed “raise to 12 cm” and “3 to 4 cm left” as given and stopped when the arm disagreed. Stopping instead of guessing is the right failure mode; it just needs a tool that turns what it sees into a move.

B1

My retry brief was wrong on the one number that mattered. “Raise to about 12 cm” caused the refusals in run 2. The successful run ignored its own advice and stayed at 8.

What to change

Ranked by how much they lower the bar for a small model. Effort is for someone who knows arm-daemon; each item is independent.
