02 · MCP: any model drives the same arm

When the AI is wrong, it's told exactly how.

refused
nothing moved, and the reason why
frozen
contact or a human takeover: held where it stopped.
not_aligned
object not between the jaws. Jaws stay open.
touch
hit something. Say what. Never repeat the move.
lost
object left the view. Look, re-point, retry once.
empty / slipped
release, re-align once, then ask the human.
3
jaw closes, then the AI must stop and ask
31
